01 · About

Quality shaped by code.

I’m Suhaib, a QA Engineer who moved from building complex web systems to owning the confidence behind their release.

Since 2022, I’ve worked across enterprise CMS platforms, NGO systems, authentication, complex forms, accessibility, and payment ecosystems. That path, from developer to QA, gave me a systems-level view of quality. I can speak with engineers, challenge requirements, trace issues through APIs, and design tests around the risks users actually face.

02 · Manual QA

Automation scales quality. Manual testing discovers it.

I investigate the product as a user, analyst, and engineer before deciding what should be automated.

My manual QA workflow
Evidence driven
  1. 01
    Understand requirements and risk

    Clarify acceptance criteria, user intent, integrations, and failure impact.

  2. 02
    Design focused coverage

    Create scenarios for core flows, edge cases, boundaries, states, and permissions.

  3. 03
    Explore and capture evidence

    Execute across browsers and conditions while recording clear, reproducible findings.

  4. 04
    Triage, retest, and release

    Communicate impact, verify fixes, run targeted regression, and support UAT decisions.
